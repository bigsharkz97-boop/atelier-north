About

Atelier North makes a small range of clothes meant to be worn for years, not seasons.

Fewer, better pieces

Each season we release a short edit of essentials. If we would not wear it on repeat, it does not make the cut.

Responsible fabrics

Organic cotton, Tencel, linen and recycled cashmere. We share where each fabric comes from on its product page.

Made to last

Every piece is repairable. Send it back and we will mend it, or take it in for recycling when it is worn out.

Contact

Questions about sizing, orders or repairs? Write to us and we will reply within two working days.
